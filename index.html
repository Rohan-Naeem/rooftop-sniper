    How to play

    You are a sniper on a rooftop. Look through your scope and stop the thieves who pop out of the windows. Thieves wear a black mask and a striped shirt. Civilians have a visible face and a colored shirt. Do not shoot them.

    Game modes

    • Time Attack: choose 30, 60 or 90 seconds and score as many points as you can. Hitting a civilian costs you 4 seconds.
    • Endless: you have 3 hearts. You lose one heart each time you shoot a civilian. Thieves that escape do not cost a heart, but they break your combo. The longer you last, the faster the targets come.

    Controls

    • Move your mouse to aim. On a phone or tablet, tap where you want to shoot.
    • Left click to fire. Right click to reload. When your bullets run out, the rifle reloads by itself.
    • Press R or right click to reload early. On a phone or tablet, tap the Reload button.

    Scoring

    • Body shot: 100 points. Headshot: 200 points.
    • Stop thieves in a row to build a combo and multiply your points.
    • Shooting a civilian costs you 150 points.
